> "I keep getting caught flat-footed at the net. What should I do?"

Marco: That's exactly what cost you points last match — and it's fixable.

The culprit is almost always the split-step. As your opponent is about to make contact with the ball, you need to do a small hop so you land just as they hit. That landing loads your legs and lets you push off in any direction instantly. Without it, you're static, and the ball is already past you.

Two things to check:
- **Timing** — are you split-stepping *as they hit*, not before or after?
- **Racket height** — is your racket up at chest height, ready? If it's down by your hip, you've already lost the race.

[LESSON_REF: ready-position | "The Ready Position"] covers both of these in detail — it's exactly where you should be right now given you haven't completed any lessons yet.

Try this in your next session: before you even think about winning the point, just focus on doing a split-step every single time your opponent swings. Nothing else. How does that sound?
